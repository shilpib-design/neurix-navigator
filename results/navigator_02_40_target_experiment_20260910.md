## SECTION 8 — KROGER CAPABILITY BOUNDARIES

1. **Kroger Without ZIP/Location Requirement**: **Context.dev API** is highly effective (100% genuine acquisition, ~1.5s latency). Standard PDP HTML is returned cleanly.

2. **Kroger With ZIP/Location Requirement**: **Local Donut CDP Browser Profiles** are strictly mandatory. Forward proxy tunnels (GeoNode/DataImpulse) are dropped at Kroger's edge, whereas local CDP instances with pre-initialized `x-active-modality` cookies succeed.

3. **Unsupported / Uneconomical Paths**: Headless forward proxy browser connections to Kroger PDPs consistently fail with edge drops (`TIMEOUT`) and should be excluded from router policies.
